01Why Celestory

You earned every photon.

Clear nights are rare and hard-won. The hours you spend chasing a target deserve more than a folder full of forgotten subs.

Every session you battle light pollution, dew, drift and your own sleep to add a few more hours onto a target. That work scatters across drives and years — thousands of frames almost no one ever sees.

Celestory reads it all back and gives it shape: every object you’ve pointed at, every filter you’ve stacked, every hour of integration — drawn into one continuous account of your time under the sky.
