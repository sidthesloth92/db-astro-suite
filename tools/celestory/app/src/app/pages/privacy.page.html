Privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Celestory is a free, open-source part of DB Astro Suite. This page explains what it collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions? Write to {{ contactEmail }}.

The Celestory CLI

The CLI runs on your computer. It reads only the headers of your FITS files (never the image data), writes one celestory.json summary, and makes no network connections. Your FITS files and folder paths never leave your machine.

Opening your file on this site

Your journey is drawn in your browser; the file itself isn't uploaded. To count the community, the site records three totals (hours, frames and number of targets) together with a random install ID and a fingerprint of those totals. None of this includes your name or your files.

Publishing a profile

Publishing is optional. If you publish, we store your summary and show it publicly at your profile link: your handle, display name, targets, equipment, filters, imaging dates and totals. Your password and recovery code are stored only as one-way hashes, so nobody can read them back.

Protecting against abuse

To stop things like password guessing, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address with a request count for up to two days. We never store the address itself.

Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to see which pages and features people use. It doesn't load until you accept, and you can change your mind at any time. Google’s own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

What’s stored in your browser

Your login for managing a profile (until you close the tab), the display name and username you set in a preview, the planetarium location if you choose one, and your analytics choice. Google Analytics cookies are only set if you accept.

Who runs the site

The website is hosted on Vercel and the database on Neon. These providers may process data outside your country.

Your choices

  • Delete your profile at any time: log in on your profile and choose Delete profile.
  • Change your handle or display name with Edit profile.
  • Decline or withdraw analytics with Analytics settings.
  • Ask us to remove anything, or report a profile that impersonates someone, through the contact page or at {{ contactEmail }}.

Changes

If this changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
