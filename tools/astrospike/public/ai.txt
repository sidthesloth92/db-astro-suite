# AstroSpike

AstroSpike is a free web application for astrophotographers that adds realistic
diffraction spikes to astrophotography images — entirely in the browser.

## What it does
You upload an astrophoto and AstroSpike detects the stars automatically, then renders
configurable diffraction spikes on them — from the classic four-point Newtonian look to
refractor-style crosses. The finished image exports as JPEG or PNG. All processing is
100% client-side: your image never leaves your browser and is never uploaded to a server.

## Core Features
- Automatic star detection
- Diffraction spike presets with adjustable parameters
- High-quality JPEG and PNG export
- 100% client-side processing — no uploads, no server, full privacy

## Tech Stack
Angular 21 (Standalone Components, Signals), TailwindCSS 4, Canvas-based
client-side image processing
