# AstroSpike

AstroSpike is a free web application for astrophotographers that adds diffraction
spikes to astrophotography images — entirely in the browser.

## What it does
You drop in an astrophoto and AstroSpike detects the stars automatically, then renders
diffraction spikes on them: the four-armed Newtonian look, a subtler variant, the
six-armed JWST signature, or a spike-free Diffusion bloom. Spike length and intensity follow each star's measured
brightness, and each spike takes its colour from the star it belongs to. The finished
image exports as PNG or JPEG at its original resolution. All processing is 100%
client-side: your image never leaves your browser and is never uploaded to a server.

## Core Features
- Automatic star detection with hot-pixel, satellite-trail, and diffuse-blob rejection
- Four style presets: Subtle, Classic (4-spike Newtonian), JWST (6-spike), and Diffusion
  (a spike-free soft-filter bloom mode)
- Spike length and intensity scaled by measured star brightness; colour sampled per star,
  with a chromatic gradient along each arm (cool root, red tip)
- Global controls: star count, length, chroma, diffusion, brightness, rotation, 4/6 toggle
- Per-star controls: click to include or exclude, double-click to tune one star alone
  (length, brightness, rotation, its own diffusion), place stars detection missed, drag a
  detection onto the star's true core
- Before/after comparison divider
- PNG and JPEG export at the source image's original resolution, plus a spikes-only
  transparent PNG layer for compositing over 16-bit masters
- Opens on a bundled Pleiades (desktop) or Cygnus Wall (mobile) sample image
- Handles 60+ megapixel frames: downscaled preview, native-resolution export
- 100% client-side processing — no uploads, no server, no account, works offline

## Tech Stack
Angular 21 (Standalone Components, Signals), TailwindCSS 4, framework-free TypeScript
image-processing functions on Canvas, with star detection in a Web Worker
